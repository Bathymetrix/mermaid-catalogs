 -they are derived from "finalized" origins published in ISC or NEIC catalogs

Matching note
-------------

OBS_TRAVTIME <--> tobs is not a direct value copy.

cfneic preserves the observed arrival UTC by modifying travel time when the
event origin changes:

    arrival_time = event_time + tobs

Therefore:

    new_tobs = old_event_time + old_tobs - new_event_time
